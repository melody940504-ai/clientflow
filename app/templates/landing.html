Questions, answered

Before you open a workspace.

Do clients need access to the studio dashboard?

No. Clients use a separate portal or public review link, so internal studio controls stay private.

Can a project keep more than one version?

Yes. Each upload remains connected to its own feedback and status, preserving the path to the approved cut.

What happens after a version is approved?

The approved version and its timeline become read-only, keeping the recorded decision intact for final delivery.

Can I try Lumaire without changing demo data?

Yes. The studio and client demos are shared, read-only workspaces with a seeded review history.
